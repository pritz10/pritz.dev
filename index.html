          help FAQ
          
Questions, answered.

        

      

      
Is hosting included?
Setup is included. Hosting and domain are billed by the provider, usually ₹1,500 to ₹5,000 a year for small sites.

      
Can I change the design after approval?
Yes, within your plan’s revision rounds. Bigger changes are quoted at the hourly rate.

      
Do I own the code?
Yes. After final payment, the code, design files and content are fully yours.

      
What if my project doesn’t fit a plan?
Most don’t fit perfectly. Message me on WhatsApp or Gmail and I’ll give a fixed quote within 24 hours.
